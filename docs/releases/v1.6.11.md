# Grindset v1.6.11

## Focus hours on the Focus page

- While Focus hours are on, the Focus page shows a card with when they end and how many rules are blocked right now.
- Later in the day, it shows when Focus hours start.

## Clearer warnings

- Warnings on the Focus page are now cards. Tapping one opens the page where you fix it: a limit opens Rules, a tracking problem opens Settings.
- Rules close to their daily limit show as "YouTube: 4 min left today", even when budget notifications are off. The **Budget warnings** setting now only controls the once-a-day notification.

Your rules, data and permissions stay as they are.
