# Grindset v1.6.21

## Rules from your account block during focus

- Rules that came to a device from your account didn't block during Focus hours or focus sessions there, only their daily limits. For example, rules set up on your phone let websites open on your PC during Focus hours. They now block during focus, the same as rules you make on the device.
- After updating, rules that arrived this way are switched on once. A rule you turned off for focus yourself stays off.

Your rules, data and permissions stay as they are.

## Publication

Published 2026-11-28 as `v1.6.21` on `danarrigo/grindset-releases` from source `d050c1b`, signed run `46117615724` (all jobs passed; the signed Android job was rerun after Maven Central answered 429 Too Many Requests). The bug came from a Windows install whose three rules (Social media, Video and streaming, Games) had arrived from the account with blocking during focus off, so websites opened during Focus hours; its `focus_flags` named none of them. Tests reproducing that state failed before the fix and pass after: a rule new to a device now blocks during focus, rules never chosen on the device are switched on once, and a rule turned off on the device stays off. Not yet tried on that install. The website APK (versionName 1.6.21, versionCode 2173) keeps the release certificate and contains no accessibility service. The Play bundle (`app.grindset`) is signed with the same certificate. All five public assets were downloaded back and matched `SHA256SUMS.txt` before `latest.json` was switched to 1.6.21.
