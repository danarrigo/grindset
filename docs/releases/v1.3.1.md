# Grindset v1.3.1

## Stronger Windows app restrictions

- Restricted native apps are minimized when opened or restored into foreground, rather than merely putting a warning in front of them.
- The explanation covers the app's entire monitor, without a movable title bar or sidebar. Closing/minimizing that frame does not dismiss the active restriction.
- Foreground changes trigger immediate checks, so returning through Alt-Tab reapplies enforcement. Idle accounting no longer suppresses an active app restriction.
- **Use other apps** dismisses the screen without allowing the restricted app. Allowed apps remove the topmost presentation and restore Grindset's previous window state.
- **Allow this app for 5 minutes** restores the restricted window after persisting its source-specific override. Existing rules, budgets, history, sign-in and update identities are preserved.

No app processes are killed or suspended, and no global keyboard/foreground policies are changed. Windows recovery controls and deliberate quitting remain available. This is not tamper-proof kiosk/security software. Apps that refuse minimization are reported as unblocked.
