# Grindset v1.6.1

## New look

- **New logo:** a block G with a green crossbar replaces the bracket icon on Android, Windows, the tray and the website. On Android 8+ it fills the launcher's icon shape, and Android 13 themed icons get a one-colour version.
- **One brand colour:** green, the colour of **Lock in**, replaces blue for links, text buttons, selected tabs and finished setup steps. Amber and red now only mean warnings and errors.
- **Calmer navigation:** the selected sidebar item on desktop is neutral grey instead of a coloured block.
- **Easier to see:** outlines on buttons and inputs are darker, meeting 3:1 contrast in light and dark mode.

No behaviour, data or permission changes from v1.6.0.
