# Grindset v1.6.13

## See all your devices in Activity

- Activity can show **All devices**, **This device**, or one of your other devices, for today and for weekly insights. With all devices, time on two devices at once is counted once, and an app and its website (the YouTube app and youtube.com) are one row today.
- Weekly insights now includes the past week from your other devices once you are signed in.

## Focus hours on the Focus page

- While Focus hours are on, the Focus page counts down to when they end, with what is blocked, instead of offering a session you do not need.

## Fixes

- Android: a blocked app no longer escapes its block screen by opening it again from Recents.
- Windows: Settings shows whether Grindset starts with Windows, with a button to turn it back on.
- Browser extension 1.5.1: the extension has the Grindset icon instead of a blank one.

Your rules, data and permissions stay as they are.
