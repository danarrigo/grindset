# Grindset v1.6.6

## Stricter blocking

- The **Allow for 5 minutes** button is gone from every block screen (Android, Windows and the browser extension). A block lasts until its focus session, schedule or daily limit ends.

## Block whole categories

- Rules offers nine categories: Social media, Messaging, Video and streaming, Games, Shopping, News and reading, AI chat, Dating, and Music and audio. Each becomes one rule with a shared daily limit, and you can remove apps before saving. They replace the mixed **All social media** button.

## Zen and Firefox

- The browser extension now works in Firefox-based browsers such as Zen, as well as Chrome and Edge. Zen Browser is in the app list.

## Also

- **Forgot password?** in Account & cloud opens a page to reset it by email.
- **Delete account** in Account & cloud deletes your account and synced data.
- Android: **Go home** on the block screen no longer leaves a block over the home screen.

Existing rules, data and permissions stay as they are. Rules made with **All social media** keep working unchanged.
