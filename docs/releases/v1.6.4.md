# Grindset v1.6.4

## Real logos

- Services and websites now show their real logo, even when the app isn't installed on this device. Installed apps keep their own icon.
- Each logo is fetched once from the service's own website (for example YouTube's from youtube.com), kept on the device and refreshed monthly. No icon service or other company is involved, and requests carry no account data.
- The sharpest logo a site offers is used. Logos that would be invisible on the tile are skipped.
- A few sites refuse these requests (for example LinkedIn and ChatGPT); they keep their letter tile.

No data, permission or sync changes from v1.6.3.

## Verification

Checked against 32 real sites: 28 returned a logo within about 9 seconds, and the tiles were reviewed in light and dark mode. All 158 existing Flutter tests pass. A bug that would have stopped every logo from loading was found and fixed during this check.
