# Grindset v1.6.18

## Ask the AI for a few minutes

- Block screens on Android, Windows and the browser can ask the AI for a few minutes in that one app or site, for example to check a LinkedIn notification or watch a video for class. Say why, and the AI decides how long, up to 30 minutes. It works in strict mode too. The app or site opens again when the time is up.
- A blocked link opens where it was going once the AI says yes.

## New look

- Block screens say "Not now, YouTube." with the app's icon, a green bar for how far through the block you are, and when it ends. Back to work sits at the bottom.
- Setup opens on a preview of a focus session, and you pick apps by tapping their icons.

Your rules, data and permissions stay as they are.
