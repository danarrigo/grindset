# Grindset v1.6.19

## The AI is only for block screens

- Asking the AI for a few minutes from a block screen no longer makes you wait first, even with a pause set in Settings. The AI is the check.
- Changing settings during a block (Focus hours, rules, breaks, ending early) keeps your pause and the "why" box, but no longer goes to the AI. Its yes would have been permanent.
- The Ask AI first switch is gone from Settings.

Your rules, data and permissions stay as they are.
