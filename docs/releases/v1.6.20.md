# Grindset v1.6.20

## A new Focus page

- A bigger, bolder timer, with 15, 25, 50 and 90 minutes to tap right under it. Other lengths are still in the menu.
- Every app and site a focus session keeps shut is shown as a pile of icons. While you focus they fade, with how many are shut.
- While a session runs, a green bar shows how far through you are and when it ends. Ending early is now a small button instead of the biggest one.
- Today's focus so far sits above the button: how long and how many sessions.

Your rules, data and permissions stay as they are.
