# Grindset v1.6.22

## Settings

- Settings is one column of groups: Tracking, Blocking, Notifications, General, Account & cloud, Data on this device and App updates. Each setting is a row saying what it does, with its switch or buttons beside it, and a whole switch row can be clicked.
- Once everything in Setup works, the checklist folds to one line. It opens again if a step breaks.

## Warnings on Windows

- "Tracking was interrupted" is gone. It showed after every sleep or restart and said nothing useful.
- A tracking problem on Windows is now one notification when it starts, instead of a notice at the top of every page. Settings in the sidebar gets a red dot while something needs fixing.

## Site logos

- Sites with big home pages, such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Supabase, now get their logo.
- A site that didn't answer, for example while the PC was waking up, is asked again the next time Grindset starts, instead of showing a letter for three days.

Your rules, data and permissions stay as they are.
