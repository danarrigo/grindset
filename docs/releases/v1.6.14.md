# Grindset v1.6.14

## Pause before unlocking

- Settings has **Pause before unlocking**: Off, 30 seconds, 1 minute or 5 minutes. While something is blocking, loosening it (turning off Focus hours, deleting or editing a rule, ending a focus session early) first asks **why do you need this?** and makes you wait. Leaving Grindset starts the wait again.
- With the pause on, the Focus menu can **take a 15-minute break**: blocking stops on this device and comes back by itself. Tap the notice to end the break early.
- Activity shows how many times you unlocked today, with your reasons.
- Strict mode still wins: while it is on, nothing can be unlocked.

## Ask AI first

- An optional switch under the pause. An AI (DeepSeek) reads your reason and only unlocks for a real need. "Bored" gets a no, with why, and another wait before you can try again.
- It needs an account and internet. Without an answer, blocks stay on. Your reasons are sent to DeepSeek and are not stored by Grindset. The [privacy policy](https://danarrigo.github.io/grindset-releases/privacy/) has the details.

## Other changes

- Turning Focus hours off keeps their times, so turning them back on brings back the same hours.
- Activity: rows with a limit have an **Edit** button, and the buttons line up.
- Weekly insights says which device it is showing.

Your rules, data and permissions stay as they are.
