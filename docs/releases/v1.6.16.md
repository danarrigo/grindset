# Grindset v1.6.16

## Setting up another device

- The first-run setup has **I already have an account**. Sign in there and setup skips picking apps, since your rules come from your account, and goes straight to the permissions this device needs.

## Even lighter on Windows

- While Grindset is hidden in the tray or minimized, it no longer redraws its window every second. Blocking and tracking keep working as before.

Your rules, data and permissions stay as they are.
