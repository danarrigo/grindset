# Grindset v1.6.7

## Easier to add apps

- On Android, the app picker lists every app on your phone by name and icon. Search finds them by name, so you never type an app ID or web address.
- **You use these most** comes first: your top apps and sites from the last 7 days, with their time. Whole browsers are not suggested, since blocking one blocks every site.

Your rules, data and permissions stay as they are. Listing your apps uses only what Android already lets Grindset see for app names and icons; nothing leaves the phone.
