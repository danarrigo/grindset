# Grindset v1.6.9

## Focus hours

- **Rules → Focus hours**: set times when every rule that blocks "While I'm focusing" is on, no timer needed. Shared across your devices.

## Strict mode

- **Settings → Strict mode**: while you focus, during Focus hours, or once a limit is reached, you can't end the session early, delete or loosen rules, or stop Grindset. Making rules stricter always works.
- On Android, the Stop button in the notification is ignored and uninstalling apps is paused. On Windows, Quit is unavailable and Grindset reopens if it is ended. A browser without the Grindset extension is blocked.

## Smaller things

- New rules are named after the apps you pick.
- **Limit** next to any app in Activity creates a rule in one tap.
- The time left on a daily limit now always adds up.
- Fixed: with instant blocking, the block screen could come back over the home screen after **Go home**, so it had to be closed twice.

Update all your devices before turning on Focus hours or strict mode: older versions can't receive them.

Your rules, data and permissions stay as they are.
