# Domain shortlist

Research date: 2026-11-12 UTC. No domain was purchased, registered or added to a cart.

## Recommendation

If keeping **Lockin**, quote **`lockinlocal.app`** first. It preserves the name and highlights the local-first positioning without limiting the product to a timer. **`lockinflow.app`** is a shorter, broader alternative.

However, choose the public brand before purchasing: multiple existing Android focus/blocker apps already use LockIn or Lockin. A different domain suffix does not resolve product confusion. This search is not trademark clearance.

## Existing same-category brands

These first-party developer listings substantially overlap the proposed product:

- **Kuuloa LLC, “LockIn: App Blocker & Focus”**: app blocking, focus timer, recurring schedules and local session history. <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.kiroku.lockin&hl=en&gl=US>
- **Monolab Co., “LockIn: App Blocker & Focus”**: app/site blocking, focus sessions, usage insights and on-phone data. <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.lockin.blocker&hl=en&gl=US>
- **Vastin Technologies, “Lockin: Focus Timer & Tasks”**: focus sessions, optional app blocking and local-first operation. <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.vastin.lockin&hl=en&gl=US>
- **“LockIn: Screen Time Control”** on iOS advertises distraction blocking: <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lockin-screen-time-control/id6756734456>.
- **`trylockin.app`** already markets a Lock In habit/journaling product: <https://www.trylockin.app/> and <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lock-in-reset-your-life/id6746586837>.

These are developer-published claims, not independent testing. This lightweight search does not establish trademark ownership, priority, jurisdiction or legal availability.

`lockin.so` remains **unverified**: no `.so` RDAP service was listed in the bootstrap and the registry website connection failed. Do not present it as available. Registry reference: <https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/so.html>.
